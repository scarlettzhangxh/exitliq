METHODOLOGY — how the verdict is made

We pull up to 41 days of hourly candles (GeckoTerminal) plus live flow (DexScreener) and replay a level state machine: a level is born when price closes ≥2% above the running all-time-high of the window. Four hours later we check back — holding ≥85% of the breakout confirms the level (a buyer layer stood its ground); sinking ≤60% is a round-trip (that layer got dumped on). Confirmed levels, buy pressure, green-day streak, volume trend and retention combine into a 0–100 structure score. A recent round-trip with no new level after it = you'd be the exit. Nothing here predicts the future; it reads the present honestly. Wrong verdicts happen — we'd rather be checkable than comfortable.
